Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Summary

Warm Wanderings does not require an account. Your detailed exploration history is stored in the app's local database on your device. The app uses Apple location services and Mapbox maps to provide its core features.

Location and local history

If you grant permission, Warm Wanderings receives precise or approximate location from iOS to reveal map areas, calculate visit and dwell patterns, estimate distance, and continue recording in the background. The resulting exploration database is stored in the app sandbox. You can deny or change location access in iOS Settings. Deleting the app deletes this local database.

Original readings and optional timeline

Alongside authorized location tracking, Preserve complete observations defaults on. It stores the location readings delivered by iOS, including coordinates, times, accuracy, altitude, speed, course and inferred visits. Available sensor readings include activity classifications, step counts, distance and floors, pressure, altitude and compass readings. Recording context includes permission state, app state and service errors. Motion & Fitness permission is required for some services. The app does not start accelerometer, gyroscope or processed device-motion recording.

You can pause original readings in Settings → Visit history without deleting saved readings or stopping the explored map. The separate detailed timeline defaults off and begins when you enable it. Dated map activity continues while that timeline is off. iOS controls sensor availability and background delivery; saved observations do not establish continuous presence or a complete route.

Personal iCloud backup

If you separately connect iCloud Sync in Settings → iCloud Backup, the app saves and merges recovery snapshots in your personal iCloud Drive. These snapshots include explored areas, visit and dwell totals, dated map activity, optional timeline entries, deletion markers and losslessly compressed original-reading archives. They may sync to your other devices using the same iCloud account. Warm Wanderings does not operate a server or developer account for this data. Apple processes iCloud data under its privacy terms. App data is also eligible for device backups according to your Apple backup settings.

Mapbox

Mapbox supplies map imagery and mapping software. Its embedded software privacy manifests declare collection of non-linked precise or coarse location, a user identifier, product interaction, other usage data, performance data, and other diagnostic data for app functionality and analytics. Mapbox declares that this data is not used for cross-app tracking. See the Mapbox Privacy Policy and the Mapbox attribution control in the app for details and telemetry choices.

Apple services

Warm Wanderings uses Apple's Core Location framework. It may also send a coordinate to Apple's reverse-geocoding service to display a human-readable place name. Apple processes that request under its own privacy terms.

No advertising or data sale

Warm Wanderings has no advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not use app data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Local history and original readings have no automatic expiry or offloading. Settings → Visit history lets you delete chronological history for selected dates or all dates; this also removes matching original readings and dated map activity locally, while lifetime explored areas, first/last visit dates and heat totals remain. Deletion markers are applied during future sync and restore to prevent older snapshots from restoring intentionally deleted history. Recording remains in its current state.

Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not erase personal iCloud snapshots, archived readings or device backups. Older cloud files can still contain deleted records. Original-reading cloud archives are not automatically pruned, and in-app permanent erasure of those old cloud files is not available. Manage remaining cloud files and device backups through Apple's iCloud and backup controls. Mapbox and Apple control retention of data they process under their respective policies.

Children

Warm Wanderings is not directed to children and does not knowingly create accounts or profiles for children.

Changes

This policy may change as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date and, when appropriate, in the app or its App Store listing.

Privacy Policy

Updated November 12, 2026

Summary

Warm Wanderings does not require an account. Your detailed exploration history is stored in the app's local database on your device. Optional Cloud Sync, which needs an account, also keeps a copy on Warm Wanderings' server so you can view it on the web. The app uses Apple location services and Mapbox maps to provide its core features.

Location and local history

If you grant permission, Warm Wanderings receives precise or approximate location from iOS to reveal map areas, calculate visit and dwell patterns, estimate distance, and continue recording in the background. The resulting exploration database is stored in the app sandbox. You can deny or change location access in iOS Settings. Deleting the app deletes this local database.

Original readings and optional timeline

Alongside authorized location tracking, Settings → Visit history → Keep original readings defaults on. It stores the location readings delivered by iOS, including coordinates, times, accuracy, altitude, speed, course and inferred visits, with the permission state and requested accuracy at the time. Warm Wanderings does not request Motion & Fitness access and does not record activity, step, floor, pressure, compass or other sensor data. Test builds before 1.5 (23) could save such readings; deleting history or Erase All Data removes them.

You can pause original readings in Settings → Visit history without deleting saved readings or stopping the explored map. The separate detailed timeline defaults off and begins when you enable it. Dated map activity continues while that timeline is off. iOS controls background delivery; saved observations do not establish continuous presence or a complete route.

Personal iCloud backup

If you separately connect iCloud Sync in Settings → iCloud Backup, the app saves and merges recovery snapshots in your personal iCloud Drive. These snapshots include explored areas, visit and dwell totals, dated map activity, optional timeline entries, deletion markers and losslessly compressed original-reading archives. They may sync to your other devices using the same iCloud account. iCloud Sync does not use Warm Wanderings' server. Apple processes iCloud data under its privacy terms. App data is also eligible for device backups according to your Apple backup settings.

Optional Cloud Sync and web companion

Cloud Sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings → Cloud Sync. It needs an account: you sign in with your email address and a one-time code. Before the first upload the app tells you that your map and timeline, including precise locations, will be stored on Warm Wanderings' server. Cloud Sync then copies explored areas, visit and dwell totals, total distance, dated map activity, timeline entries, deletion and erase markers, four map display preferences and a stamp summary to that server, and merges changes from your other devices and the web companion. The stamp summary holds an area-based level and your counts, the date you started exploring, and the stamps you earned with their dates. It also lists the countries and territories you stamped or passed through, with how much of each you explored. It has no coordinates. Cloud Sync does not upload original readings, your last location, GPX files or recording settings.

The web companion shows this copy after you sign in with the same email address. It does not request your browser's location. While you are signed in, it keeps a copy in your browser; signing out deletes it. It can export your explored map, change those display preferences, delete history for selected days, erase all data and delete the account. It also offers a synthetic tour that contains no one's real history.

The server runs on Supabase, which stores your email address, a random account identifier, each synced device's random identifier, app version and last upload time, and the synced copy in a database encrypted at rest; connections use TLS. Each account can read only its own data. This data is used only to provide Cloud Sync and the web companion. It is not sold, used for advertising or sent to analytics.

Mapbox

Mapbox supplies map imagery and mapping software. Its embedded software privacy manifests declare collection of non-linked precise or coarse location, a user identifier, product interaction, other usage data, performance data, and other diagnostic data for app functionality and analytics. Mapbox declares that this data is not used for cross-app tracking. While usage analytics is off, including before you leave the first setup screen, the app turns Mapbox telemetry events off; Mapbox still receives the map requests needed to show the map. See the Mapbox Privacy Policy and the Mapbox attribution control in the app for details and telemetry choices.

Optional local notifications

With your permission, the app schedules notifications on this iPhone for earned stamps and levels, a weekly progress recap, and tracking-health reminders: a check after a day without app or location activity, spaced follow-ups for up to 90 days, and checks from about 15 minutes after saves or location readings start failing repeatedly. These reminders do not diagnose whether tracking failed. Recaps show the progress available when last updated; iOS controls background execution and delivery. Notification content is not sent to a push service or analytics provider. Each category can be disabled in Settings, and place names are hidden by default.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

If you add a widget, the app writes a small summary on this iPhone for it: this week's time at stops, visits, new ground, your stamp count, and the name you gave your top place, if any. Lock Screen widgets show that name only when Show place names in notifications is on. The summary never contains coordinates or addresses, is not sent anywhere, and is deleted by Erase All Data.

Apple services

Warm Wanderings uses Apple's Core Location framework. It may also send a coordinate to Apple's reverse-geocoding service to display a human-readable place name. When you open a place under Stats → Your places, the app sends the center of that map cell to Apple Maps to list businesses within about 60 meters, and keeps the results on this iPhone for 30 days. Names you give places, and the places you hide, stay on this iPhone and in its device backups; Erase All Data deletes them. Apple processes these requests under its own privacy terms.

Third parties

The only third parties that receive data from the app are Mapbox (maps), Segment by Twilio and Google Analytics (usage analytics, only while sharing is on), and Apple (location, reverse geocoding, place search and iCloud). If you use Cloud Sync or the web companion, Supabase (hosting, database and sign-in), the email provider that delivers sign-in codes, and Mapbox (web maps) also receive data. The web map loads Mapbox tiles for the area you view, so Mapbox can tell which area that is. The web turns Mapbox's performance telemetry off. Mapbox's required map-load events, with a random identifier that changes daily, still reach Mapbox. Each processes that data under its own privacy terms (Mapbox, Twilio Segment, Google, Apple, Supabase) and provides the same or equal protection of user data as this policy and Apple's App Store guidelines require.

No advertising or data sale

Warm Wanderings has no advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not use app data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Local history and original readings have no automatic expiry or offloading. Settings → Visit history lets you delete chronological history for selected dates or all dates; this also removes matching original readings and dated map activity locally, while lifetime explored areas, first/last visit dates and heat totals remain. Deletion markers are applied during future sync and restore to prevent older snapshots from restoring intentionally deleted history. Recording remains in its current state.

Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not erase personal iCloud snapshots, archived readings or device backups. Older cloud files can still contain deleted records. Original-reading cloud archives are not automatically pruned by date-based history deletion. In build 1.5 (18) and later, Settings → Erase All Data can erase this device's exploration stores and delete the app's snapshots and original-reading archives from the connected iCloud account, leaving a small erase marker to prevent older snapshots from restoring erased data. The app reports whether cloud deletion succeeded or was unavailable. This does not remotely erase other devices' local copies, device backups, exported files, analytics already received by providers, or files retained in iCloud Recently Deleted. Manage those copies through the relevant device, provider or Apple backup controls. Mapbox and Apple control retention of data they process under their respective policies.

Cloud Sync keeps the cloud copy until you delete it. History deleted on a synced device or on the web is removed from the cloud copy, and each iPhone applies the deletion at its next sync; a large deletion waits for confirmation there. Erase All Data, on the iPhone or the web, erases the synced map, totals, dated map activity, timeline, deletion markers and stamp summary of one Cloud Sync account: the one the iPhone uses, or the one signed in on the web. It leaves an erase marker so a device that was offline cannot upload erased data again. Your display preferences, device records and sign-in stay until you delete the cloud account. Copies in other Cloud Sync accounts used on the iPhone stay until they are erased or deleted there, for example on the web. If you are signed out of Cloud Sync when you erase on the iPhone, the cloud copy is erased after you sign in again. An iPhone that still holds data asks before erasing its own copy. Erase This Device Too, which applies another device's iCloud erase, also erases the Cloud Sync copy this iPhone uses, for every device on that account, the same way. Settings → Cloud Sync → Delete Cloud Account, or Delete account on the web, deletes the cloud copy and the account. Other signed-in devices sign out the next time they sync, and data on your devices stays. Turning Cloud Sync off stops syncing and keeps the cloud copy. Deleted cloud data can remain in the provider's encrypted backups until they expire.

Children

Warm Wanderings is not directed to children and does not knowingly create accounts or profiles for children.

Contact and deletion requests

For privacy questions and data deletion requests, email privacy@brantleybeaird.com; for support, email support@brantleybeaird.com or use the Warm Wanderings support page. Your history on your devices, in your iCloud Drive and in the Cloud Sync account the iPhone uses is deleted with Settings → Erase All Data, without contacting us. Settings → Cloud Sync → Delete Cloud Account deletes the rest of your Cloud Sync data and the account. For usage analytics already received by Segment or Google Analytics, email us with the approximate dates; because the app's identifiers change every launch, we may not be able to single out your events, and we will tell you what we could delete.

Product usage analytics

Usage analytics sharing is on by default on the first setup screen; you can turn it off there or any time in Settings → Privacy → Usage analytics → Share usage analytics. Nothing is sent before you leave that screen. When sharing is on, the app sends fixed screen names, onboarding outcomes, map layer and control choices, harmless map preferences, help navigation, fixed failure and retry codes, and backup, restore and export outcomes to Segment, which forwards them to Google Analytics. Events include the app version and build, launch time, bounded duration or latency categories, and a random identifier that changes every launch. Backup, restore, export and onboarding attempts use temporary random identifiers to avoid counting duplicate callbacks. These identifiers are not saved on the device or shared across apps.

The app does not send coordinates, map cells, place names, travel or visit history, stamp details, permission or tracking state, filenames, file paths, account or folder identifiers, exported contents, or raw error messages through this analytics adapter. While sharing is on, capped diagnostic counters for tracking, permissions, timeline, stamps and local notifications stay only in the app process's memory; they are erased when analytics is disabled or the app exits. They are never sent to Segment or Google Analytics.

Turning analytics off cancels pending requests, prevents future collection and also turns off Mapbox telemetry events. It does not delete events already received by providers. As with any web request, Segment receives the network connection from your IP address and ordinary request metadata; the app asks Segment not to record the IP address with events. Analytics is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. App identifiers are temporary, so these reports do not establish cross-visit retention or uniquely identify people.

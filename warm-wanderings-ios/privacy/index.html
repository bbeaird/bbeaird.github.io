Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

Summary

Warm Wanderings does not require an account. Your detailed exploration history is stored in the app's local database on your device. The app uses Apple location services and Mapbox maps to provide its core features.

Location and local history

If you grant permission, Warm Wanderings receives precise or approximate location from iOS to reveal map areas, calculate visit and dwell patterns, estimate distance, and continue recording in the background. The resulting exploration database is stored in the app sandbox. You can deny or change location access in iOS Settings. Deleting the app deletes this local database.

Mapbox

Mapbox supplies map imagery and mapping software. Its embedded software privacy manifests declare collection of non-linked precise or coarse location, a user identifier, product interaction, other usage data, performance data, and other diagnostic data for app functionality and analytics. Mapbox declares that this data is not used for cross-app tracking. See the Mapbox Privacy Policy and the Mapbox attribution control in the app for details and telemetry choices.

Apple services

Warm Wanderings uses Apple's Core Location framework. It may also send a coordinate to Apple's reverse-geocoding service to display a human-readable place name. Apple processes that request under its own privacy terms.

No advertising or data sale

Warm Wanderings has no advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not use app data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

The local exploration database remains until you delete Warm Wanderings or erase its data through iOS. Mapbox and Apple control retention of data they process under their respective policies.

Children

Warm Wanderings is not directed to children and does not knowingly create accounts or profiles for children.

Changes

This policy may change as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date and, when appropriate, in the app or its App Store listing.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the Warm Wanderings support page.

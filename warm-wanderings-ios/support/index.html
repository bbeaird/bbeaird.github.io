Support

Warm Wanderings is an early-stage app built by Brantley Beaird. If something is confusing or not working, please report it with the iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include screenshots or exports containing private location history unless you intentionally want to share them.

Contact

Contact Brantley through brantleybeaird.com. Please include “Warm Wanderings” in your message.

Location troubleshooting

Open iOS Settings → Apps → Warm Wanderings → Location. Choose Always for background recording and enable Precise Location for accurate street-level exploration. Leave the app in the app switcher; swiping it away prevents iOS from relaunching it for location events until you open it again.
